Caring for a Pug

A thoughtful routine helps Pugs stay comfortable, active, and ready for plenty of companionship.

Offer measured meals and fresh water, and keep treats balanced with daily activity. Pugs can gain weight easily, so a healthy body condition is especially important for their breathing and joints.

Short walks and gentle play are usually better than strenuous exercise. Avoid hot, humid conditions and watch for heavy panting, weakness, or distress because Pugs can overheat quickly.

Clean facial folds carefully and keep them dry. Regular brushing, nail trims, dental care, and veterinary checkups also help prevent small problems from becoming uncomfortable ones.

Every dog is different. A veterinarian can give the best advice for an individual Pug's diet, exercise, breathing, eyes, and overall health.
