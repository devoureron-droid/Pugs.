Personality and appearance

What makes a Pug a Pug?

Pugs are compact, expressive companion dogs with a big personality packed into a small body.

Pugs have a square, sturdy build, a short glossy coat, a tightly curled tail, and a distinctive wrinkled face. Their large, expressive eyes and playful expressions are part of their unmistakable charm.

They are generally affectionate, sociable, and eager to be around people. Most Pugs enjoy family life, gentle play, and plenty of attention, making them devoted companions for individuals and households alike.

Although they can be lively, Pugs are usually adaptable and do not need intense exercise. They are happiest with a balance of short activities, mental stimulation, and comfortable time with their people.

Pugs are also intelligent and can learn quickly when training is patient, positive, and consistent. Their curious nature and love of attention often make them responsive to praise and rewards.
